Cities & Holdings ​

Tap one of your cities and the city board opens straight away — with or without a party selected on it. Cities level from 1 to 3 — each level adds job slots, a floor to the skyline, and eventually unlocks the best projects.

Jobs ​

Spare Followers work the five city jobs: the Market (gold), Tavern (elixir), Temple (reagents), Magistrate (infamy) and the Farms (2 food each — the best-paying plot in the city). Infamy matters: your deeds plus your magistrates decide how many new Followers arrive every seventh day.

Food & farms ​

The faithful march on bread, not coin: every recruit costs 1 Follower, 40 food and 20 elixir — gold buys mercenaries, structures and training, never the faithful. Cities grow a base crop (villages a smaller one), Farm workers grow more, and for real granaries you settle farmsteads: hire a Farm Hand at any of your cities (100🪙), march them out with a party, and on held, empty ground the command bar's 🌾 slot lights up to raise the farmstead. The hand settles there for good; the farmstead yields 10 food a day at full control — and like any holding of yours, its fences ward the blight. A ring of farms is both a food supply and a wall.

Training ​

Every unit standing at the building — in a party or the garrison — can buy its next level for gold and elixir. The drillmaster only charges for what is left to learn: a soldier already three-quarters of the way to the next level pays a quarter of the list price, so battle scars are money in the bank. Training takes the day; the queue shows who's drilling. Keep a high-level veteran garrisoned and new recruits start at half their level.

Barracks train warriors, Laboratories train alchemists, Shadow Guilds train rogues; cities and Grand Athanors drill warriors. Tap any owned trainer building on the map to open its hall directly.

Build ​

Cities can raise new structures on free land beside them — a Barracks, Laboratory or Distillery, and at city level 3 a Shadow Guild. One project at a time; a few days each.

Research ​

The Town Hall researches hero skills, one project at a time, guild-wide once learned. Class skills are innate; a level‑2 city unlocks the deeper studies (Shield Wall, Plunder, Field Mending) and a level‑3 city the masterworks (Warcry, Overwhelm, Quicksilver March). Once researched, assign the skill on any matching hero's sheet. The full tree lives in Skills & Research.

Defense ​

If a visible enemy prowls within sight of an undefended holding, the game warns you and offers an emergency muster — recruits or mercenaries straight onto the walls. The locate button's jump list shows every city, flagging any whose walls stand empty.
