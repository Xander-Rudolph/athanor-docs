The Bestiary ​

The alchemists of old did not stop at metals. Their creations — and their escapes — still walk the world.

Creature lairs ​

Five great lairs stand in the wilds, each heavily warded and each home to one of the great creatures:

LairCreatureFights like
Golem FoundryBrass GolemAn anvil with fists — huge, armored, slow
Salamander VentSalamanderA walking furnace — searing ranged fire
Basilisk PitBasiliskThe crowned serpent — venom at speed
Quicksilver WellQuicksilver ElementalLiving mercury — faster than any rider
Chimera VatsChimeraThree beasts stitched into one fury

Capture a lair and you may hire its creature — for a fortune up front and a ruinous daily gold upkeep. Miss a day's pay and the creature rampages: it mauls its own party for half their health, then abandons you. Feed your monsters.

The Wilds ​

Packs of Homunculi — vat-spill given legs, the little horrors of the wastes — roam the world and hunt any party they find, sometimes with an escaped creature among them. They belong to no guild, take no gifts, and cannot be tamed.

The Beastmaster ​

Any city grown to level 3 attracts a Beastmaster — hire one into a party from the command bar's Beasts slot (200🪙 and a Follower).

They are no soldier. A Beastmaster barely swings, and carries only daggers and accessories: their craft is the snare. What they are worth is what they bring back alive.

  • After a won battle, each downed creature has a chance of being taken alive and joining you, upkeep and all. Homunculi are never worth keeping; the Beastmaster won't even try.
  • Setting a snare — the command bar's 🪤 Set Trap, on open ground away from any holding — baits something out of the wild. It costs the whole day's marching, and there is no declining it: the fight opens at once, and the quarry moves first.

On the field the Beastmaster gains a 🪤 Snare action against a beast standing next to them. The weaker the beast, the likelier it goes into the sack — wear it down first, but do not kill it: a dead beast ends the battle with nothing to show. A successful snare ends the fight then and there and the beast is yours.

Trapper's tools — snare-cords, torcs, muzzles — are accessories that add to those odds, and they only ever turn up as loot for a party that keeps a Beastmaster.

Taming ​

A beast learns no trade. What it earns instead is tameness — it gets used to its keepers — and a settled beast is far cheaper to feed. Keep one in a Beastmaster's company and it settles a little further every day.

Each kind also brings a trick nobody teaches it:

BeastIts own art
ChimeraVenom — the bite keeps working for three more turns
BasiliskPetrifying Gaze — one look and they cannot take a step
SalamanderEmber Breath — fire over a three-tile square
Quicksilver ElementalQuicksilver Surge — strikes, then moves again
Brass GolemBrazen Guard — +3 guard to itself and everyone beside it

Homunculi can be snared too. They are vat-spill and they learn nothing, but they have legs and they can be made to carry a spear.

The Bestiary ​

Raise a Bestiary in a city's ring (220🪙 and 20💎 at a level-2 city): a keepers' hall with stalls, a paddock and a feed trough. Post Followers to it as you would to any craft.

A level-3 city with four keepers posted to its Bestiary opens beast lore for research — skills no hall will teach you:

SkillWhat it does
Beast BondEvery beast in the hero's party fights at +3 attack and +3 defence
Wild HeartBeasts feed for half the gold and mend 15% of their health a day
Quickening BalmShared with the alchemists — see below

These belong to a Beastmaster raised to Champion, and to no one else. A Beastmaster can be promoted like any other veteran.

The Quickening Balm ​

An alchemist's study (researched at a level-2 hall) that a Beastmaster shares. On the battlefield it is laid on ground, not on a body, and soaks in three battlefield turns later — turns of the fight, not of your own — healing everyone standing in that patch when it settles.

The delay is the point: you are committing to where your wounded will be, not where they are. In a Beastmaster's hands the balm tends only the beasts.
