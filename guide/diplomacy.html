Diplomacy & Trade ​

Guild relations ​

Every rival guild holds an opinion of you, from Loathing to Devotion. Attacks and captures sour it; gifts of gold sweeten it. Guilds that bear you no grudge leave your lands alone — war is something you earn, by drawn blades and sour demands. Your sworn rival is the exception: capped at Hostility forever, they come for you regardless. And Entropy answers to none of it: its armies never range beyond the creeping edge of the blight, but where the rot has reached, they spare no one.

Parley & barter ​

Parley opens an audience with a rival lord. Gifts always work; full barter opens once they feel Empathy toward you. Build a deal from both ledgers — what you offer against what you request — and watch the scale: they'll call it Insulting, Stingy, Fair or Generous.

Your stance matters too. A Plea needs the sweetest deal, a Request is the honest middle, and a Demand extracts more but sours their opinion whether or not they accept. Generous deals warm it.
