Skills & Research ​

Where each art is taught ​

The Town Hall teaches one art per class per tier — the old three. Everything else is taught where the trade actually lives, which is a reason to raise those structures beyond drilling in them:

BuildingWhat it teaches
BarracksBlinding Dust, Bulwark
LaboratoryQuickening Balm, Vitriol Flask, Quicken
Shadow GuildShadow Step, Assassinate, Poison Blade
BestiaryBeast Bond, Wild Heart, Ensnare

Nothing is innate

Your Lord begins with steel and a title, not a technique. Every skill is studied at a Town Hall — including your own class's art, which is simply the cheapest project on the list and the one to start with.

Every hero carries up to three skills. The first is the innate art of their class, locked in from the day they take command; the rest are earned in the study and assigned from the unit sheet.

How research works ​

Research happens at the Town Hall of any city you own; the hall's level is the city's level, and it gates what can be studied:

Hall levelOpensCostTime
1The class skills (innate, free)——
2The deeper studies (tier 2)150🪙 10💎5 days
3The masterworks (tier 3)200🪙 16💎6 days

One project sits on the bench at a time, guild-wide. Once a skill is mastered it belongs to the whole guild forever: any hero of the right class can assign it from their unit sheet, from that day on. A mastered skill can be unassigned and reassigned freely; only the innate class skill is fixed.

The warrior's line ​

SkillTierWhat it does
📣 Rally1 · innateThe whole party fights at +2 attack and +2 defense while this hero leads
🛡 Shield Wall2The party braces at +3 defense whenever it is the one attacked
📯 Warcry3The enemy line falters: every foe fights at −2 defense

Warriors anchor a line. Rally is always on; Shield Wall turns a garrisoned warrior hero into a wall worth breaking armies on; Warcry is the siege-breaker, softening a deep garrison before the blades cross. Rally and Warcry together swing every exchange by four points.

The rogue's line ​

SkillTierWhat it does
🌫 Ambush1 · innateA free opening volley whenever this party attacks, +15% to a fleeing Lord's escape, and ambushes are sprung more often
🪙 Plunder2Hoards turn up in 60% of taken holds instead of 40%, and dungeon chests run half again as rich
⚔ Overwhelm3The class counter (warrior beats rogue beats alchemist beats warrior) deals double damage instead of half again, for the whole party

Rogues profit from initiative and greed. Ambush makes every attack begin a step ahead; Plunder pays for itself within a few raids, and it belongs on whichever hero storms the most holds; Overwhelm rewards building a party that counters what your enemy fields. Rogue heroes also sneak parties past dungeon floors, and flee down stairs mid-fight.

The alchemist's line ​

SkillTierWhat it does
⚗️ Transmute Blast1 · innateOpens battle by burning half the hero's Essence as damage that ignores armor (on the tactical field, a cast at range 3)
🩹 Field Mending2The whole party heals an extra 10% of max HP every day, even on the march
🌀 Quicksilver March3The hero's craft lightens every boot: the party marches +3 move points a day

Alchemists win wars between the battles. Transmute Blast scales with Essence, so a leveled alchemist Lord opens with a thunderclap; Field Mending keeps a campaigning army whole without ever going home; and Quicksilver March makes one army fight like two by arriving first. Put March on the party that answers threats, and Mending on the one that never stops pushing.

Reading the research screen ​

Open any city's Research tab: each skill shows its class, tier, cost, and state. Class skill means innate; mastered means done and assignable; on the bench is the current project. A skill grayed with "needs hall 2/3" wants the city upgraded first, and upgrading any one city to level 3 opens the masterworks for the whole guild.
