AthanorThe war for the blighted heart of the world

A turn-based strategy game of alchemy, guilds, heroes, and the Great Work — inspired by the 1997 classic Lords of Magic.

Get Started
How to Play
🗺️

A globe at war

Seven guilds ring Entropy's vast central blight and fight inward. Circumnavigate a world that wraps in every direction — the deeper you press, the harder the fights and the finer the treasure.

⚔️

Battles your way

Auto-resolve skirmishes in a tap, or fight them out on an isometric tactical grid — ambushes, dungeon floors, sneaky descents and all.

👑

Heroes worth raising

Level your Lord, promote champions, collect artifacts, learn skills, tame the alchemical bestiary — and keep everyone paid, or else.

A love letter to Lords of Magic: Special Edition (1997).
